Before you play

The kitchen

You and a partner cook onion soup in a small kitchen. Neither of you can finish a dish alone in the time you are given, so you must divide the work.

What to do

  • Pick up an onion and put three of them in a pot.
  • Wait for the soup, then take a dish and fill it.
  • Carry the dish to the grey square to deliver it.

The keys

←↑↓→ Space

The arrow keys move you. The space bar picks a thing up and puts it down. One press is one move.

You play two rounds with two different partners. After the second round we ask you which partner you preferred.

You can read this again at any time.
